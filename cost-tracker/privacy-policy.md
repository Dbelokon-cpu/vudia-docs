# Privacy policy: Cost Tracker for Jira

Effective date: 17 October 2026

## Who we are

Vudia provides Cost Tracker for Jira ("the app"). Contact for
privacy questions: our privacy email (to be published here shortly).

## What the app does with your data

The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, inside your Jira Cloud
site. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and the app does not
send your data to us or to any third party. We cannot see the data your site
holds in the app.

The app reads, from your Jira site and with the permissions of the person using
it: issues in the scopes you define (key, summary, status, type, dates), the
worklogs on them (who logged how much time and when), Jira project lists and
user names. It stores, in Forge storage for your site: the projects, hourly
rates, roles, expenses, budgets, portfolios, accounts and report settings that
your users enter, plus temporary cached report totals. Hourly rates linked to
named people can be sensitive; who can see them is controlled by the sharing
settings of each project.

## Personal data

Atlassian account ids and display names of people who log time, own projects or
are added as members; the hourly rates entered for them; and any free text
users type (expense descriptions, customer and contact fields).

## Why

Only to provide the app's features for your organisation: cost, budget and
profit reporting from Jira time tracking. No advertising, profiling or sale of
data.

## Sharing and transfers

We do not receive or share your data. Data is processed and stored by
Atlassian as the Forge platform provider; see Atlassian's own privacy and Forge
data-residency documentation for where and how.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays in your site's Forge storage until your users delete it in the
app or the app is uninstalled; removal after uninstall follows Atlassian's
Forge retention. Site admins control who can use the app.

## Your rights

Because we hold no copy of your data, requests to access, correct or delete
personal data are handled by your Jira site administrators inside the app and
Jira. If you contact us, we will explain how, at our privacy email (to be published here shortly).

## Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
