# Support: Cost Tracker for Jira

- **Contact:** our support email (to be published here shortly)
- **Hours / response target:** business days
- **Include in a report:** your Jira site URL, the app version (Apps, Manage apps), what you did, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot. Please do not send rates or other sensitive figures unless we ask.

## Common questions

**A project shows no cost.** Cost comes from worklogs on issues matching the
project's scope. Check the scope JQL in Settings and that the issues have
logged time. The report is cached for 15 minutes; press Refresh to rescan.

**"Capped at 500 issues".** A scope is read up to 500 issues. Narrow the JQL or
split the project.

**A person shows the default rate.** They have no rate of their own, through
their role or through a cost account. Set one on the Team tab.

**I cannot open or edit a project.** Access is per project (owner, editor,
viewer, or restricted). Ask the owner, or a Jira site admin, to change sharing.

**Revenue tab is empty.** Turn on Revenue tracking or Fixed price in the
project's Settings; time-based projects have no revenue.

**Where is my data?** In Forge storage on your Atlassian site; see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
