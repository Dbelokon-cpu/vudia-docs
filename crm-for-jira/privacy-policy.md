# Privacy policy: CRM for Jira

Effective date: 17 October 2026

## Who we are

CRM for Jira ("the app") is provided by Vudia. Contact for privacy questions: our privacy email (to be published here shortly).

## What the app does with your data

The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, inside your Jira Cloud
site. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and the app does not
send your data to us or to any third party. We cannot see the data your site
holds in the app.

The app stores, in Forge storage for your site, the records your users enter:
leads, contacts, companies, deals, products, tasks, notes, saved views,
settings, and an audit trail of changes. It reads from your Jira site, with the
permissions of the person using it: user profiles (to show names and check that
an owner or assignee is a real user), work items your users link to records
(key, summary, status), and whether the person is a Jira administrator.

## Personal data

- About your customers and prospects, as your users type them: names, job
  titles, email addresses, phone numbers, LinkedIn addresses, company details
  and free-text notes.
- About Jira users: Atlassian account ids of record owners and assignees, of
  who created or changed a record, and of who wrote a note or appears in the
  audit trail. Jira users' names are looked up when shown, not stored.

Your organisation decides what customer data to enter and is responsible for
having a lawful basis for it.

## Why

Only to provide the app's features for your organisation: managing sales
leads, customers and deals in Jira. No advertising, profiling or sale of data.

## Sharing and transfers

We do not receive or share your data. Data is processed and stored by
Atlassian as the Forge platform provider; see Atlassian's privacy policy and
Forge data-residency documentation for where and how.

## Atlassian accounts that are closed

Once a week the app reports the Atlassian account ids it holds to Atlassian's
Personal data reporting API. When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the
app removes that id from every record (owned records become unassigned) and
deletes that person's saved views.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays in your site's Forge storage until your users delete it in the app
(Settings → Delete All Data removes everything) or the app is uninstalled;
removal after uninstall follows Atlassian's Forge data-retention policy. The
audit trail keeps the most recent 500 entries.

## Your rights

Because we hold no copy of your data, requests to access, correct or delete
personal data are handled by your Jira site administrators inside the app. If
you contact us, we will explain how, at our privacy email (to be published here shortly).

## Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
