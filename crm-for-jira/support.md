# Support: CRM for Jira

- **Contact:** our support email (to be published here shortly)
- **Hours / response target:** business days
- **Include in a report:** your Jira site URL, the app version (Apps → Manage
  apps), the section you were in, what you did, what you expected, what
  happened, and a screenshot. Please do not send customer data unless we ask.

## Common questions

**The Dashboard does not show my latest change yet.** Dashboard and Reports
are worked out from a summary refreshed every few minutes. The line under the
title says when; press Refresh to update it now.

**A section opens slowly.** Each section loads its records when opened, and is
designed for up to 5,000 records. Settings → Data size → Count Records shows
how many each section holds.

**I cannot find a contact in a picker.** Pickers find records whose name, or a
person's surname, starts with what you type.

**I cannot change the currency or the stale threshold.** Workspace settings
are for Jira administrators.

**A CSV import row failed with "no company named …".** Imported deals and
contacts link to an existing company or contact by exact name. Create it
first, or correct the name in the file.

**Where is my data?** In Forge storage on your Atlassian site; see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
